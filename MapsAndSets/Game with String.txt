# Minimize String Value After K Removals

### ✅ Problem Statement

You are given a string `s` and an integer `k`. In one operation, you can remove a character from the string. Your goal is to perform exactly `k` such operations in a way that minimizes the sum of squares of the frequency of each character in the resulting string.

---

### 💡 Intuition

If we want to **minimize the sum of squares** of character frequencies, we should always reduce the **highest frequency character** first — because squaring amplifies higher numbers more.

Example:
- "abbccc" → freq = {a:1, b:2, c:3}
- Squared sum = 1² + 2² + 3² = 14
- Remove 1 `c` → freq = {a:1, b:2, c:2} → sum = 1 + 4 + 4 = 9

---

### 🧠 Approach

1. Count the frequency of all characters in the string using a `unordered_map`.
2. Perform `k` iterations:
   - Each time, reduce the character with the **highest frequency**.
3. After all removals, calculate the sum of squares of remaining frequencies.

---

### 🕰️ Time and Space Complexity

- **Time Complexity**:
  - Frequency Counting: O(N)
  - Each Removal: O(26) → worst case constant
  - Total: O(N + K × 26) ⇒ Efficient for small `k`

- **Space Complexity**: O(1) (since only lowercase letters are used, max 26 keys in map)

---

### 🧪 Example

```cpp
Input:
s = "abbccc"
k = 2

Output:
9

Explanation:
- Remove one 'c': {a:1, b:2, c:2} → sum = 1 + 4 + 4 = 9
- Remove one 'b': {a:1, b:1, c:2} → sum = 1 + 1 + 4 = 6
